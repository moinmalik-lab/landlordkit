Notice periods are not optional

Most states require 30 to 60 days' written notice before a rent increase or a decision not to renew. Miss the window and the lease usually rolls over on the existing terms, whether you meant it to or not. That's why the amber band here starts at 60 days.

Renewing beats re-letting

A turnover costs a month of vacancy, cleaning, listing time and screening. A renewal at a modest increase almost always nets more than a new tenant at a higher rent. Run the numbers in the rental yield calculator before deciding.
