Move-in / move-out record

Room-by-room condition, recorded at both ends of the tenancy. This is the document that decides deposit disputes.

Signatures

Landlord signature & date

Tenant signature & date

Do this on day one, not on the way out

Deposit disputes are decided by whoever can show the condition at the start. A signed move-in record, dated and with photographs attached, ends the argument before it begins. Most states also require you to return a deposit within 14 to 30 days with an itemised list of deductions — and several impose penalties of two to three times the deposit for missing that deadline. Print two copies, both parties sign, tenant keeps one.
