The number most people get wrong

Gross yield ignores every cost, which is why listing sites quote it. A property showing 8.6% gross can be losing money each month once tax, insurance, vacancy, maintenance and the mortgage are counted. Judge a deal on cash-on-cash return and monthly cash flow — those are the two you actually live with.
