How this calculator works

The break-even rent accounts for the fact that your unit won't be occupied 100% of the time. If your vacancy rate is 8%, you're empty roughly 1 month per year — so you need to charge enough rent during the occupied months to cover all 12 months of costs.

Formula: Break-even rent = Total monthly costs ÷ (1 − vacancy rate) ÷ (1 − management rate). Always set rent above this number to ensure positive cash flow.
